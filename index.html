Illustrative league

One week in a sector league.

  1. Draft the market.

    Take turns in a snake draft. Each stock belongs to one team, so if NVDA goes before your pick, you need a new plan.

  2. Set your lineup.

    Pick eight starters before Monday’s open. In a sector league every slot is a position: Tech, Healthcare, Financials and more, plus two Flex spots.

  3. Your stocks against theirs.

    Slot against slot, your starters play your opponent’s for the week. Points come from how each stock actually moves. Higher total wins.

  4. Climb the standings.

    Every week counts toward your record. The top four make the playoffs, and one team takes the trophy.

Your league, your rules.

Two settings, chosen when the league is created. Any combination works.

Draft

Draft any stocks you like. All-in on tech is allowed.

Every slot is a sector, like positions on a team, plus two Flex spots.

Budget

You split the weekly budget and can trade during the week.

The budget splits evenly at Monday’s open and stays put all week.
