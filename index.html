Illustrative league

  1. Draft

    Draft the market.

    Take turns in a snake draft. Each stock can only be on one roster, so if someone takes NVDA before you, you need a different plan.

  2. Lineup

    Set your lineup.

    Before the week starts, pick who starts and who sits. In a sector league, every slot is a position: Tech, Healthcare, Financials, Consumer, Industrials, Energy, plus two Flex spots.

  3. Matchup

    Go head to head.

    Your starters play your opponent's starters, slot against slot. Points come from how your stocks move that week, and the higher score takes the win.

  4. Standings

    Climb the standings.

    Every week counts toward your record. The top teams make the playoff bracket, and one of them takes home the trophy.

Fantasy settings

Your rules.

Draft style

Draft any stocks you like. Eight starters, no restrictions. Going all-in on tech is allowed.

Each slot is a sector, like positions on a football roster. Fill them all, with two Flex spots for anything.

  • SLOTNVDA
  • SLOTMSFT
  • SLOTAAPL
  • SLOTAVGO
  • SLOTAMD
  • SLOTMETA
  • SLOTTSM
  • SLOTGOOGL

Portfolio style

You get a weekly budget and decide how much goes into each starter, and you can trade during the week.

The budget splits evenly across your starters at Monday's open. No trading, no rebalancing. Pick your lineup and watch.
