How a season works

A season, start to finish

  1. Before week 1

    Draft

    Take turns picking real stocks and ETFs in a snake draft. Every pick is yours alone for the season.

  2. Each weekend

    Set your lineup

    Start the stocks you believe in and bench the rest. Claim free agents and trade with other managers.

  3. Monday – Friday

    Matchup

    Your starters play your opponent's. Points come from how much your lineup gains that week.

  4. End of season

    Playoffs

    The top records make a bracket. One team takes the trophy, and everyone gets a season recap.

Fantasy leagues

The draft, weekly matchups and playoffs described above.

Classic leagues

Everyone starts with the same practice cash and trades any stock. The best portfolio at the end wins.

Solo

No league needed. Build a portfolio and track your trades on your own.

For clubs and classes

Run your group's league

  • Who it's forInvestment clubs, business fraternities, finance classes, student-managed funds and groups of friends.
  • Getting inMembers join from an invite link or code. No setup on their end.
  • CommissionerThe organizer sets the start date, budget and draft time, and manages members.
  • LearningA trade coach and research tools show members why their picks worked, or didn't.
No real
money.

Stockyard uses real market prices with practice money. You can't deposit, withdraw, bet or win cash, and no trade ever reaches a real exchange.

It's a game for learning how markets move, with friends keeping score.
